How special blocks are detected
  • Each H2 heading starts a new content section.
  • A paragraph starting with Quick Summary: becomes the summary box at the top.
  • A paragraph starting with Pro Tip:, Note: or Learn More: becomes an info box.
  • An H2 named FAQ (or "Frequently Asked Questions") starts the FAQ. Lines ending in ? are questions; the text after each is its answer.
